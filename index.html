Problem statement

Millions of small businesses in India still have no website, so customers cannot find or trust them online.

The problem

  • Customers search Google first, but many local shops do not appear.
  • Prices, timings and address are not available online.
  • Paper menus and pamphlets are costly and go out of date.
  • Owners cannot afford expensive agencies.

Our solution

  • An affordable, fast website made with HTML and CSS.
  • One link and QR code to share on social media.
  • Email and Maps buttons in one tap.
  • Free hosting on GitHub Pages or Netlify.

Questions

How long does a website take?

A Starter site takes about 2 days. A Business site takes about 5 days after you send photos and details.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Every site is responsive and tested on small screens first.

Who pays for hosting?

Hosting on GitHub Pages or Netlify is free. A custom domain name costs extra, about ₹800 a year.

Can I change prices later?

Yes. Prices live in simple text and can be changed quickly.
